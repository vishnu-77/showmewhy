# ShowMeWhy brand mark

The ShowMeWhy mark is a modern Socratic thinker rather than a literal historical bust.

## Hidden meaning

- **Thinking pose** — scrutiny before acceptance.
- **Three flowing hair paths** — evidence can arrive through different routes; the middle path quietly forms an `S` for ShowMeWhy.
- **Inner profile** — a second voice inside the larger face, representing dialogue, challenge and the Socratic question: *how do you know?*
- **Open negative space around the face** — room for a claim to remain `OPEN`; the mark does not imply certainty by default.
- **No question-mark or verification badge** — questioning and proof are encoded in the construction rather than added as decorative symbols.

The canonical assets are:

- `showmewhy-mark.svg` — icon/favicons/CLI and compact uses.
- `showmewhy-lockup.svg` — README, website and wider brand surfaces.

The mark should remain monochrome: near-black on paper/off-white, or the inverse on dark backgrounds. Avoid gradients, glow, glossy finishes and decorative colour.
